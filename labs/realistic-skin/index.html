Realistic skin lab

Skin is not one effect. It is a stack: light scattering under the surface, an oily top layer with two specular lobes, pores, uneven color from blood and melanin, and light leaking through thin parts. Switch each layer on and off.

Try this

  1. Press Mannequin, then turn layers back on one at a time, top to bottom. Each one removes a different "this is plastic" cue.
  2. Set Split compare to 50% and orbit the head.
  3. Press Nose shadow close-up and switch the diffuse model between Lambert and the two scattering models. Watch the shadow edge turn soft and red.
  4. Turn Dual-lobe off: the highlight on the forehead and nose becomes a single hard blob. On: a sharp core with a wide soft sheen.
  5. Press Back-lit ear, then toggle Transmission.
  6. Raise Melanin: darker skin shows scattering less and specular more, because less diffuse light comes back out.
