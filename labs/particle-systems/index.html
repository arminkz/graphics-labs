Particle systems lab

A particle is a position, a velocity and an age. Each frame: spawn new ones, move them with a few forces, retire the old ones, then draw every one as a small textured square. Fire, smoke, sparks and snow are the same system with different numbers.

Try this

  1. Press each preset and compare the numbers: the code is the same, only the settings change.
  2. With Sparks, switch simulation to GPU and drag Particles to 1 M. Switch to CPU: it caps at 100 k and the step time shows why.
  3. With Smoke, pick the Hard disc sprite and toggle Sort: unsorted alpha blending makes far puffs draw over near ones. Then switch to Additive: order no longer matters.
  4. Turn off Soft particles with Smoke or Fire: a hard line appears where each square cuts into the ground.
  5. Open the Overdraw stage and raise Size at death: a few big particles cost more than thousands of small ones, because the cost is pixels covered.
  6. With Smoke, switch Particle layer between full and half resolution and watch the fps. Smoke is soft, so the lower resolution is hard to see. Try the same with Sparks: thin streaks get blurry.
  7. With Fire, set Turbulence and Air drag to 0: the flame becomes a rigid cone. Forces are what make it look alive.
  8. Press Burst with Sparks for an explosion.
