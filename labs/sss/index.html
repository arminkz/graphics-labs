Subsurface scattering lab

Light that enters skin, wax or jade bounces around under the surface and comes out somewhere else. Here that is approximated after lighting: blur the diffuse light across the screen, by a real-world distance, with a different width per color channel.

Try this

  1. Move Split compare to 50%: left half without SSS, right half with. The stripes on the head get soft, warm edges, and the skin stops looking like plastic.
  2. Open the Diffuse light stages 4 → 5 → 6: horizontal blur, then vertical. Only the diffuse light is blurred. Albedo and specular stay sharp.
  3. Pick Naive screen blur and zoom in and out: the blur stays the same number of pixels, so the face looks waxy from far away and barely blurred up close. It also bleeds onto the floor.
  4. Back on separable, turn off Follow the surface and look where the head overlaps the background: light leaks across the edge.
  5. Turn off Per-channel profile: the scattering turns gray and the face looks like soft plastic. The red fringe is what reads as skin.
  6. Press Close-up on the ear with the back light on: thin parts glow red because light passes through them.
