Try this

  1. Pick Crytek: the flat floor and walls turn gray, because half of every sphere is under the surface. Switch to hemisphere and they turn white again.
  2. Turn off Random rotation: smooth but banded rings. Turn it on with Blur None: grainy 4×4 pattern. Then pick 4×4 box: the pattern averages away.
  3. Turn off Range check and look where the table and blocks overlap the far wall: dark halos around their outlines.
  4. Drag Cube height to 0 and back up: the contact shadow under the cube grows and fades.
  5. Set Radius to 2.5 m: AO turns into big dirty blobs. At 0.2 m only tight creases darken.
  6. Move Split compare to 50%: left half without AO, right half with it.
