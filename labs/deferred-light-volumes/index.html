Try this

  1. Set View to Cost heatmap, then step through modes 1 → 5 and watch the runs count drop.
  2. Turn on Solo, set Volumes to x-ray and View to Useful vs wasted. In mode 3, the red parts of the footprint are surfaces in front of or behind the sphere.
  3. Click the thumbnails under the scene to see each stage full size. Stage 5 splits light #1's footprint into the A/B/C cases.
  4. Pick mode 2, press Put camera inside light #1, then switch to mode 3. Mode 2's light vanishes because its front faces are behind the camera.
