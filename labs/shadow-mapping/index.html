Try this

  1. Press Show acne and look at the ramp and the ground: stripes everywhere. Raise Depth bias or Slope-scale until they disappear.
  2. Press Show peter-panning: the table and spheres float, because their shadows no longer touch them.
  3. Set Resolution to 256 and Filter to Hard, then open the Texel grid stage to see why the edges get blocky.
  4. Turn off Stabilize cascades, turn on Slowly orbit the camera, and watch shadow edges shimmer. Turn stabilization back on.
  5. Pick PCSS and raise Sun size: the table legs' shadows stay sharp where they touch the ground and blur further away.
