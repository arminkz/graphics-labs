Try this

  1. Click a spot on the floor. The yellow line shows where its reflected ray landed on the screen. The cyan dot is the pixel whose color it borrows.
  2. Open the Hit UV stage: every reflective pixel is colored by where its ray hit. Black means no hit.
  3. Orbit to look steeply down at the floor. Reflections vanish: the rays point back toward the camera, and what's behind you was never on screen. Turn off Fade rays that point back to see the garbage that fade hides.
  4. Pan until a pillar touches the screen edge. Its reflection fades out before the edge, because the ray would need pixels that don't exist.
  5. Set Thickness to 3 m: reflections smear behind the spheres and the thin pole. Set it to 0.02 m: rays slip behind objects and leave holes.
  6. Pick the view-space march, set Step length to 1 m and Binary refine to 0: stair-stepped reflections. Raise refine to 4 to fix them for a few extra reads.
  7. Raise floor Roughness: reflections blur by reading a lower-resolution mip of the lit image, more for farther hits.
